\subsection{Panel Structure and Sample Construction}\label{sec:sample}

Two features of the panel matter for the design.
\begin{itemize}
  \item Every promotion, transfer and pay change carries an exact effective date.
  \item Division and cohort fixed effects absorb the time-invariant composition of regional offices.
\end{itemize}
