\begin{abstract}
This sample paper shows what a paper written with EcoFin.sty looks like. Its data are invented. A panel of 12,000 personnel records and 144,000 person-years is used to estimate how senior appointments change in treated divisions after a policy break. The estimate is a decline of 18.4 per cent. The paper has a table, a figure, two equations and an online appendix with its own reference list, so that each part of the style can be seen once.
\end{abstract}
